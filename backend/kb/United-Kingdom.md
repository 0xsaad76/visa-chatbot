# United Kingdom Standard Visitor Visa

The UK Standard Visitor route is for tourism, family visits, short business activities, and other limited temporary purposes. Applicants should show they are genuine visitors, can support themselves, will leave the UK at the end of the trip, and will not live in the UK through repeated or successive visits.

Strong applications include clear travel plans, stable finances, employment or study ties, evidence of family or property ties, and invitation details where relevant.
