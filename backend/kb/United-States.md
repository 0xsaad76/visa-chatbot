# United States B1/B2 Visa

The B1/B2 visa is for temporary business or tourism travel. Consular officers assess the applicant's stated purpose, ability to fund the trip, ties outside the United States, and overall credibility. The DS-160, interview answers, and supporting evidence should be consistent.

Applicants should be ready to explain their itinerary, occupation, income source, prior travel, family situation, and return plans in direct and concise terms.
