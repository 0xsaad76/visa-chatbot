# Schengen Tourist Visa

Schengen short-stay applicants normally need a valid passport, completed application form, travel medical insurance, accommodation proof, flight reservation, itinerary, employment or business evidence, and recent bank statements. Officers evaluate whether the applicant has enough funds, a credible temporary travel purpose, and reasons to return home.

Common refusal risks include inconsistent itinerary dates, unexplained large bank deposits, weak employment ties, missing insurance coverage, and unclear main destination. Applicants should make sure all dates match across insurance, hotel booking, flights, and leave letters.
